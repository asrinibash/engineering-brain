# Inbox

Raw capture. One line per item, newest at the bottom, dated. Nothing here is
organised, generalised, or trusted yet — this is the pile, not the knowledge.

Items get added mid-task by an assistant when I say "add that to the brain".
They get promoted into `knowledge/` in a separate review pass — see
`/brain-review`, or [the maintenance section in README.md](README.md).

**Rules for adding:** one line, dated `YYYY-MM-DD`, say what happened and why it
mattered. Strip company specifics on the way in — no table or DAG names,
hostnames, ticket IDs, or colleague names. If it can't be written without them,
write the general version and note that the specifics stayed in the project.

**Rules for promoting:** if it's happened in two different projects, it's
transferable and belongs in `knowledge/`. If it's only happened once, leave it
here or drop it — one occurrence might just be a quirk of that codebase.

Delete lines once promoted. This file should be near-empty most of the time; if
it's long, the review is overdue.
